FAQ

Before you go solar, know what matters.

Straightforward answers to some of the questions people ask before choosing a solar energy system.

How do I know what size solar system I need?

System size depends on the appliances you want to power, your power demand, usage pattern, desired backup duration and available installation space. Our assessment and calculator can provide an initial starting point before a more detailed system recommendation.

Can solar power an air conditioner?

Yes. Air conditioning can be included in a properly designed system. The inverter, battery storage and solar array need to account for the air conditioner's capacity, starting demand, operating hours and the other appliances being powered.

What is the difference between an inverter and a complete solar system?

An inverter manages and converts electrical power, while a complete solar system can combine solar panels, inverter equipment, batteries, protection components and the installation infrastructure required to operate them together.

Is lithium better than tubular battery?

Neither option is automatically right for every property. Lithium batteries can offer high usable capacity, long cycle life and lower maintenance, while tubular batteries may suit projects where their characteristics, operating requirements and budget are appropriate.

Can I start with a smaller system and expand later?

In many cases, yes. Expansion should be considered during the original design so the inverter, battery architecture and other system components can support future requirements where possible.

Do you provide installation and after-sales support?

Our service model is built around the complete project journey from assessment and system design through installation, commissioning and ongoing support.

Do I need batteries if I have solar panels?

Batteries store solar energy for later use and can provide backup when solar production is low or grid power is unavailable. Whether storage is appropriate depends on your usage pattern and backup requirements.

How do I start?

Start with the energy assessment to provide information about your property, appliances and backup needs. The team can then use those details to understand the system requirements.
